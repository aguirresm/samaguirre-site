Privacy policy

Last updated September 3, 2026. This notice covers Aguirre Technologies LLC websites and apps, including this site, Interior Buddy, and GymSaver. It is a product privacy notice, not legal advice.

Who we are

Aguirre Technologies LLC publishes small software products. Privacy questions: github.com/aguirresm or this site.

This website

samaguirre.com is a static site. It does not use accounts, analytics pixels, or advertising. Your browser may send a standard request log to the host (Vercel), which is subject to their terms.

Interior Buddy

Room scans, floor-plan photos, and generated layouts stay on your iPhone unless you back up the device. Camera and Photos are used only for scanning and attaching a plan. Connecting Pinterest is optional. If you connect it, Interior Buddy requests read access to public boards and pins you grant, and uses that board to infer a style. Tokens are not stored in source code. See the longer Interior Buddy notice at this policy page.

GymSaver

GymSaver has its own policy at gymsaver.app/privacy.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not scrape other people’s Pinterest boards. We do not run ads on this site.
